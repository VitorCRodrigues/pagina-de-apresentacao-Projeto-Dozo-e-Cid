# 08 — Competitors & Differentiation

> The doc that decides viability. No sugar-coating: **almost every piece of our thesis is already being done by someone better-funded.** This lays out exactly who, where they're weak, and the *narrow* ground on which we can actually win, improve margins, and take share.

---

## 1. Brutal reality check (read this first)

- **We have no technology moat at launch.** Stablecoin rails, card issuing (Pomelo), US custody (Apex/DriveWealth), KYC — all commodity, all rentable by anyone.
- **The field is crowded *and* capitalized:**
  - **Revolut** — full banking license in **Mexico (Jan 2026)**, expanding to **Brazil, Colombia, Argentina, Peru**; **70M+ customers, ~$75B valuation, $4B revenue**. A generalist juggernaut moving into *exactly* our markets with FX + card + investing + crypto.
  - **Nubank** — **100M+ users** across BR/MX/CO; USDC, premium tier (Ultravioleta), investing. Owns LatAm distribution.
  - **Bitso** — USDC yield **~4% APY**, **5,000+ US stocks/ETFs**, cross-border rails, $2.2B-valuation. **Already does most of our stack.**
  - **DolarApp / ARQ** — **2M users** (MX/AR/CO), USDC global account + **Pomelo** card. The direct dollar-access analog.
  - **Avenue / Nomad** (Brazil) — US stock/ETF investing for Brazilians, fractional from $1, FDIC/SIPC. The closest to our *investing* thesis.
  - **Belo, Lemon, Buenbit, Ripio** (AR) — stablecoin wallets + cards + yield.
  - **Wise** (cheap FX, weak LatAm on/off-ramp, no investing), **Western Union/Remitly** (remittance, trusted, expensive), **Mercado Pago** (MELI super-app).
- **Therefore:** "cheaper FX" or "USD account + card" or "buy US stocks" are all **table stakes, already shipped by funded incumbents.** Competing head-on on any single one of these is a losing game.

> **The honest conclusion:** we cannot out-Revolut Revolut or out-distribute Nubank. We win only by being the **focused specialist** in an underserved *niche* the generalists don't prioritize — then building switching costs and expanding. Anything else is delusion.

## 2. Competitive map — and where each one is *weak* (our opening)

| Category | Who | Their strength | **Their gap = our opening** |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Global neobank** | Revolut | capital, multi-country, full stack | Generalist; not LatAm-diaspora-native; trading/spending framing, **not retirement**; thin local trust |
| **LatAm incumbent** | Nubank, Mercado Pago | 100M users, distribution, trust | Domestic-resident focus; **not** cross-border-life or US-retirement focused; you're one tab of 30 |
| **Crypto super-app** | Bitso, Lemon, Ripio | USDC yield, US stocks, crypto-native | **Crypto/trading framing** scares the conservative saver; no advice, no retirement, no employer channel |
| **Dollar-account app** | DolarApp/ARQ, Belo | clean USD account + card | **Spending/holding, weak/no investing**, no retirement, single-life (resident, not diaspora) |
| **US-investing-for-locals** | Avenue, Nomad (BR); INDmoney, Vested (IN) | direct US brokerage | **Self-directed & trading-framed**; single-country; no FX/card/remittance bundle; no managed/retirement product |
| **Remittance** | Western Union, Remitly, Wise | trust, reach | expensive (WU) or **no investing/wealth layer** (Wise); transactional, not a relationship |

**The pattern:** everyone does **one slice** well. **Nobody owns "the integrated USD-wealth home for the cross-border / diaspora person who wants to escape a broken home currency and *build long-term dollar wealth*."** That gap — integration + retirement framing + diaspora focus — is the only defensible ground.

## 3. Uncomfortable truths we must accept
1. Differentiation is **positioning + integration + execution + distribution** — not tech. All copyable; speed and focus are the only protection.
2. **Yield is already competed to ~4% (Bitso)** — float income is not our margin.
3. A giant (Nubank/Revolut) *could* add "US retirement" tomorrow. Our defense is **focus, depth in a niche, and switching costs they won't bother to match for a segment they under-prioritize.**
4. Realistic ambition is **"own an underserved niche and grow it,"** not "take share from Nubank head-on." The credible big outcome is **category leadership in a niche → multi-market expansion → defensibility *or* acquisition.**

---

## 4. Our differentiation thesis (the narrow ground we can win)

Ranked by defensibility:

### 4.1 — Retirement / wealth-preservation framing (not trading, not spending) ⭐
- **Everyone else frames around trading (Bitso/Nomad), spending (DolarApp), or crypto (Lemon).** No one owns **"USD retirement for people whose home pension and market destroy wealth"** — backed by the EWZ-vs-S&P ~10x gap ([05 §7](05-Examples.md)).
- Targets the **saver, not the trader** — higher LTV, far stickier, lower churn, recurring AUM (the margin engine).
- It's also the product that **threads every regulatory needle** ([07](07-complicated-cases.md)): small recurring contributions = TCS-free in India, inside Turkey's cap, sub-quota everywhere.

### 4.2 — B2B2C: employer-sponsored USD retirement (the user's "pensions for employees") ⭐
- **Distribution moat the consumer apps don't have.** Sell USD retirement/savings plans **through LatAm employers** (tech firms, exporters, multinationals) to their staff → **near-zero CAC, payroll-deduction stickiness, bulk onboarding.**
- Payroll integration = **switching costs** (people don't leave the plan their salary funds). This is the closest thing to a real moat available to us.
- Generalists (Revolut/Nubank) are consumer-first; an employer-channel specialist can own this lane. See [04 Other Services](04-Other%20Services.md).

### 4.3 — The multi-country **diaspora** specialist
- Most rivals serve **in-country residents**; we serve the **cross-border life**: the Argentine in Miami supporting family + investing in the US + traveling; the Brazilian in Europe. **One account across countries**, vs single-country incumbents (Avenue=BR only, DolarApp=MX/AR/CO).
- Revolut is multi-country but a *generalist*; we out-serve a *specific* diaspora identity.

### 4.4 — Trust + advice + local-language service for the conservative saver
- The category is crypto-coded and opaque; the **risk-averse saver** is underserved. Transparent ownership, human Portuguese/Spanish advice, goal-based managed portfolios (vs self-directed), education. Slow to build, but compounds.

### 4.5 — Niche-first beachhead, not mass-first
- Win **one** underserved segment the giants ignore (e.g., a profession — doctors/IT workers wanting USD retirement; or one diaspora corridor) where we can be #1, then expand. (Narrowest viable wedge.)

> **One-line position:** *"The USD retirement & wealth home for emerging-market savers and their families — at work and across borders."* FX/card are the funnel and the daily utility; **investing is the product**; retirement + employer channel are the moat.

## 5. Improving margins & edges

| Lever | Effect | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **Mix-shift FX → AUM** | thin (0.3–0.5%) → recurring 0.5–1%/yr, high LTV | the funnel→engine thesis ([05 §7](05-Examples.md)) |

| **B2B2C payroll** | **CAC collapse** + retention | biggest margin lever via low acquisition cost |
| **Own more of the stack over time** | reclaim partner take-rates | agent → own license/ramps as volume funds it ([03](03-compliance-licensing.md)) |
| **Up-market services** | high fee | tax/consulting, premium advisory, eventually the **accredited hedge fund** ([05 §4.4](05-Examples.md)) |
| **Closed-loop P2P** | cost avoidance | user-to-user transfers skip on/off-ramp cost; also a network effect |
| **Yield on balances** | **commoditized (~4%)** | table stakes, *not* a differentiator — don't lean on it |

## 6. Moats we don't have yet — and how we earn them
1. **Switching costs** (strongest, fastest): retirement AUM + payroll deposit + card = nobody leaves. Build from day one.
2. **Distribution lock-in**: employer/B2B2C contracts; diaspora-community + affiliate referral loops.
3. **Multi-country licensing**: the agent→own-license progression ([03](03-compliance-licensing.md)) becomes a barrier once owned across markets.
4. **Brand/trust in a specific niche**: be *the* name for "USD retirement for [segment]."
5. **Data**: cross-border diaspora underwriting/behavior the generalists don't specialize in.
6. **Network effects**: P2P send + referrals within tight diaspora communities.

We start with **zero** of these. The plan is to convert focus + execution into #1 and #2 within 18 months, then #3–#6.

## 7. Differentiating "with investors" (both meanings)

**A) To our investing *customers* (vs Bitso/Nomad/IBKR):**
- **Managed & goal-based** (retirement, education, house) — not a bare self-directed trading screen.
- **Auto-DCA + employer payroll** funding; fractional; local-language advice.
- **Wealth-preservation narrative**, not speculation — fits the risk-averse saver the others ignore.
- **Bundled** with the FX/card/remittance life they already need.

**B) To *capital* investors (the raise):**
- **Don't pitch "another LatAm neobank"** — VCs are saturated on that and you'd lose to Revolut/Nubank comps.
- Pitch: **"The USD-retirement platform for the ~600M emerging-market savers whose home markets destroy wealth (EWZ −50–70% vs S&P +400% in USD). FX is our CAC-efficient funnel; AUM + employer payroll is the high-LTV, defensible engine."**
- Lead with the **B2B2C distribution moat** and **niche dominance**, not TAM hand-waving. That's the fundable, differentiated story.

## 8. Scaling & taking share — overcoming the hard situations

| Situation | The problem | How we overcome it |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold start vs 100M-user incumbents** | no distribution | Niche beachhead + **employer channel** (bulk, low-CAC) + diaspora referral + **content marketing on the demand thesis** ("your money in IBOV vs the S&P") |
| **CAC > LTV** | paid acquisition is expensive | B2B2C payroll (CAC≈0), referral loops, organic education; convert FX users → AUM to lift LTV |
| **A giant copies us** | Nubank/Revolut bolt on "US retirement" | Be **faster + deeper** in the niche; own employer contracts + switching costs they won't chase for a segment they under-prioritize; consider **being acquired** (legit outcome) |
| **Regulatory scaling** | 1 country at a time | the reusable **chassis**, wave-by-wave ([07](07-complicated-cases.md)) |
| **Capital efficiency** | can't outspend rivals | **agent/asset-light** first ([03](03-compliance-licensing.md)); spend only behind proven unit economics |
| **Trust event** | a competitor depeg/scandal, or our own | be the **transparent, well-structured, audited** option; diversified stablecoins/custodians; it becomes a share-grab moment for us |
| **Price war** | rivals cut FX to zero | don't anchor on FX margin — anchor on **AUM + bundle + service**; FX is the funnel, not the P&L |

---

## 9. Honest verdict — what has to be true to win
1. We **pick one niche** (recommend: **retirement-savers, employer-channel-led, one diaspora corridor**) and become #1 there before expanding. ✅ controllable
2. The **retirement/AUM + payroll** model produces switching costs fast enough to survive a giant's attention. 🟡 must prove
3. **CAC stays low** via B2B2C + referral (not paid head-to-head with Revolut). 🟡 the make-or-break metric ([05 §6](05-Examples.md))
4. We accept the realistic outcome set: **niche leadership → multi-market → defensibility *or* acquisition** — not "beat Nubank." ✅ if expectations are set

If we instead launch as "another USD account + card + buy US stocks" with no niche, no employer channel, and no retirement framing, we are **road-kill** between Revolut, Nubank, and Bitso. The differentiation *is* the business.
