## 1. Executive decision

The idea remains commercially plausible, but the current memo describes a regulated, multi-product business as if it were already solved. The best solution is to reduce the first release to one corridor, one investment product, one licensed-partner chain, and one measurable conversion hypothesis.

The company should not solicit customers or move live customer money until the following six gates are closed:

1. **Brazil perimeter gate:** written Brazilian advice covering solicitation, investment advice, intermediation, advertising, the local employee's activities, and the exact licensed-partner structure.
2. **U.S. advisory gate:** the correct state/SEC RIA and IAR route, custody configuration, fiduciary conflict controls, and broker-dealer compensation analysis are documented and implemented.
3. **Partner and banking gate:** each bank, FX institution, VASP, PSP, and custodian has approved the complete business model in writing, not merely one isolated transaction.
4. **Portfolio-execution gate:** the exact UCITS instruments, exchanges, customer countries, account type, advisor permissions, recurring order workflow, and fractional-share capability have passed a live or production-equivalent test.
5. **Operational-control gate:** customer-money attribution, sanctions/PEP screening, privacy, security, daily reconciliation, exception handling, refunds, and incident response have passed a tabletop test.
6. **Economic gate:** a controlled pilot has measured CAC to a funded investor, conversion, funding delay, deposit, contribution, retention, AUM, and contribution margin. A bottom-up model must survive conservative scenarios with adequate cash headroom.

If any gate fails, the answer is to narrow, defer, change partner, or stop. It is not to compensate with more entities, more products, or more optimistic assumptions.

## 3. Closure classes

Every issue below uses one of four closure classes:

- **One-time decision:** closed by a documented choice; reopen only when the named trigger occurs.
- **Opinion / approval:** closed by a written opinion, registration, or counterparty approval tied to the exact operating facts; refresh when those facts change.
- **Build and test:** closed when an implemented control passes defined tests.
- **Ongoing control:** never permanently closed; it receives an owner, review frequency, evidence, and escalation rule.

“Closed” means the stated evidence exists. A meeting, verbal assurance, email summary, or untested contract draft does not close an item that requires formal approval or operational proof.

## 4. Chronological execution plan

### Stage 0 — Contain the thesis before spending (Days 0–5)

**Goal:** remove avoidable errors and freeze scope before they propagate into legal work, fundraising, product design, or vendor negotiations.

1. Approve a Brazil-first, investment-only MVP and Argentina fallback rule.
2. Remove the card, employer channel, multi-country tax engine, proprietary licensing, Uruguay operating entity, pooled vehicle, and VPN from the launch critical path.
3. Correct product naming, custody-versus-domicile terminology, IOF language, Uruguay claims, QSBS, R&D credit, AML timing, performance claims, and moat claims.
4. Create one evidence repository with folders for legal opinions, registrations, partner approvals, product tests, policies, model evidence, and decision records.
5. Appoint accountable roles: CEO/business owner, CCO/legal owner, operations owner, finance owner, security/privacy owner, and independent reviewer. A four-person team may outsource roles, but accountability cannot be unassigned.

**Exit evidence:** signed scope decision, corrected claims register, named owners, and a budget for Stages 1–3.

### Stage 1 — Define the legal perimeter (Weeks 1–4)

**Goal:** determine who may perform every customer-facing and money-moving action before marketing or onboarding begins.

#### 1A. Brazil operating opinion

Brazilian securities and financial-regulatory counsel should receive the complete factual workflow, including Portuguese marketing, referrals, website geotargeting, the Brazil-based team member, suitability, recommendations, order flow, fee collection, Pix, FX, VASP use, settlement, customer support, complaints, and data transfers. The opinion must answer each activity separately; “the account is in the customer's name” is not a conclusion.

Default operating design:

- The local licensed institution is the contracting and operational party of record for every regulated local step.
- The offshore company does not publicly present specific securities to Brazilian residents before compliant onboarding and suitability.
- The Brazil-based team member does not solicit, recommend, onboard, accept orders, quote FX, promise execution, or negotiate customer contracts unless counsel places the activity within an approved supervised role.
- The U.S. adviser provides advice only within the documented advisory relationship and registration perimeter.
- Contracts include a service-and-responsibility matrix for KYC, AML, sanctions, pricing, disclosures, order acceptance, execution, custody, settlement, complaints, refunds, regulatory reporting, record retention, and incident reporting.

Recent CVM proceedings illustrate that an offshore website, Portuguese support, local payment methods, app availability, and Brazil-directed advertising can be evidence of local public activity. The BCB's 2025 virtual-asset framework also brings specified virtual-asset transfers and fiat-referenced assets into the FX perimeter. See the [CVM 2026 proceeding](https://www.gov.br/cvm/pt-br/assuntos/noticias/anexos/2026/20260324-pas-cvm-19957-006709-2021-81-parecer-do-comite-de-termo-de-compromisso.pdf) and the [BCB regulatory announcement](https://www.bcb.gov.br/detalhenoticia/20918/nota).

#### 1B. U.S. advisory and compensation opinion

- Determine state versus SEC registration from principal office, clients, and regulatory assets under management; do not label the entity “Florida RIA” as if that answers the question.
- Maintain a migration calendar at $90m, $100m, and $110m RAUM. The SEC currently describes $100m as the permitted threshold, $110m as generally required, and below $90m as the general deregistration threshold, subject to exceptions.
- Register every required investment adviser representative and implement Form ADV, advisory agreements, code of ethics, compliance manual, best-execution review, books and records, marketing review, and annual compliance review.
- Configure the custodian so the adviser has only the minimum permissions needed. Document trading authority, money-movement authority, fee deduction, standing letters, account statements, and any resulting custody obligations.
- Prohibit securities transaction-based compensation, referral commission splits tied to transactions, spread shares linked to securities execution, and per-trade fees unless broker-dealer counsel approves a registered structure. Prefer a disclosed fixed or AUM advisory fee.
- Adopt a conflict inventory covering the incentive to increase remittance volume, deposits, AUM, affiliate revenue, and partner economics.

Primary references: [SEC adviser thresholds](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/capital-raising-building-blocks/investment-advisers), [current custody rule](https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-17/chapter-II/part-275/section-275.206%284%29-2), and [SEC broker-dealer registration guide](https://www.sec.gov/about/divisions-offices/division-trading-markets/division-trading-markets-compliance-guides/guide-broker-dealer-registration).

#### 1C. AML, privacy, tax, and product opinions

- Implement risk-based KYC, sanctions, PEP, source-of-funds, enhanced due diligence, escalation, recordkeeping, and transaction-monitoring controls now because partners and risk demand them. Maintain a dated activation plan for any additional FinCEN requirements due in 2028; do not misstate them as currently effective federal RIA duties. [FinCEN postponed the adviser rule to January 1, 2028](https://www.fincen.gov/news/news-releases/fincen-issues-final-rule-postpone-effective-date-investment-adviser-rule-2028).
- Complete a data map, lawful-basis analysis, notices, retention schedule, vendor DPAs, access matrix, breach plan, and cross-border transfer mechanism. Brazil's international-transfer regulation requires a valid mechanism and data minimization; see [ANPD Resolution 19/2024](https://www.gov.br/anpd/pt-br/acesso-a-informacao/institucional/atos-normativos/regulamentacoes_anpd/resolucao-cd-anpd-no-19-de-23-de-agosto-de-2024).
- Obtain a product-level U.S./Brazil tax memo for estate-tax and dividend treatment. State conclusions by ISIN, fund domicile, account owner, client domicile/residence, and custody chain. Do not promise elimination of all estate, probate, succession, withholding, or local tax consequences.
- Remove QSBS from the base case. Section 1202 excludes financial services, brokerage, banking, financing, and investing businesses from qualified trades or businesses; see [26 U.S.C. §1202](https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&num=0&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title26-section1202).
- Calculate, rather than assume, any R&D payroll tax credit. The election is capped at $500,000 and depends on actual credit, qualified-business status, and filing requirements; see the [IRS Form 6765 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i6765).

**Exit evidence:** signed legal opinions, registration roadmap, approved compensation structure, custody memo, AML plan, privacy package, and tax claim matrix.

### Stage 2 — Prove the partner chain and controls (Weeks 2–6)

**Goal:** prove that the proposed route works end to end, with a second path available for every critical vendor.

1. Obtain full-model approval from the receiving bank, custodian, PSP, FX institution, and VASP. Disclose countries, stablecoin use, transaction purpose, customer profile, volumes, ownership, advisers, and all entities. A generic onboarding approval is insufficient.
2. Negotiate commercial and risk terms: service levels, reserve rights, price-change notice, termination notice, transition assistance, data portability, audit rights, incident notification, subcontractor disclosure, complaint ownership, and regulatory cooperation.
3. Maintain a primary and credible backup for the customer-collection/FX path. Do not duplicate the whole stack; prioritize the failure points that can stop deposits or withdrawals.
4. Build an instrument capability matrix by ISIN/ticker, domicile, exchange, currency, minimum notional, fractional eligibility, recurring-order support, customer-residence eligibility, adviser-account availability, fees, and API behavior. Execute at least one production-equivalent fractional order and one whole-share fallback.
5. Build three-way reconciliation across the local partner ledger, international/virtual-asset leg, and custodian account. Every item needs a unique customer/transaction identifier, expected-versus-actual amount, timestamps, fees, FX rate, status, and exception owner.
6. Define the stablecoin policy: approved issuer and asset, approved chains, counterparty limits, wallet controls, address screening, travel-rule allocation, confirmations, chain congestion response, irreversible-transfer response, issuer depeg/freeze response, and maximum intraday exposure.
7. Run tabletop scenarios for rejected transfer, name mismatch, sanctions hit, AML hold, partial settlement, wrong reference, over/underpayment, partner outage, depeg, wallet compromise, bank account closure, and customer death.

IBKR publicly reports fractional eligibility for many U.S., Canadian, and European stocks and ETFs, including clients of financial advisers, but that does not prove that a particular UCITS instrument and account/residence combination works. Closure requires the exact-instrument test, not a marketing page. See the current [IBKR fact sheet](https://www.interactivebrokers.com/en/general/about/ibkr-fact-sheet.php).

**Exit evidence:** signed partner approvals and contracts, backup-path plan, completed instrument matrix, test trades, reconciliation report, exception log, and tabletop results.

### Stage 3 — Run a controlled evidence pilot (Weeks 6–12)

**Goal:** test the only critical commercial hypothesis: whether a remittance or FX user becomes and remains a funded recurring investor at an acceptable contribution margin.

#### Pilot design

- 20–50 invited participants; no mass advertising.
- Brazil only, subject to Stage 1 approval.
- Manual or low-code operations are acceptable if controls and records are complete.
- One model portfolio with a documented whole-share fallback.
- No card, employer integration, VPN cross-sell, second country, or automated tax filing.
- Separate cohorts by acquisition channel; do not combine founder referrals with paid acquisition.

#### Required funnel and cohort measures

1. Lead-to-KYC completion and KYC approval rate.
2. Fully loaded CAC to a customer and CAC to a **funded investor**.
3. FX/remittance use, then funded-investor conversion rate.
4. Days from acquisition to first investment.
5. Initial funded deposit and net cash actually reaching custody.
6. Monthly recurring contribution rate and amount.
7. 30/60/90-day investor retention and contribution retention.
8. AUM per funded investor, net deposits, withdrawals, and market return reported separately.
9. Gross and contribution margin by FX, investing, support, tax-reporting pilot, and partner.
10. Revenue and AUM concentration for the top 1, 5, and 10 clients.

#### Pilot go/no-go rule

Before scaling, management must set thresholds with the board or lead investor. At minimum, the model should be able to answer and withstand:

- CAC to funded investor at base, 2x, and 3x observed levels;
- acquisition growth at 5% monthly or lower, not only 10%;
- rail cost at 1.0% and partner minimum fees;
- a fully loaded fixed-cost case including founder/team compensation, legal, compliance, insurance, security, customer support, vendors, and contingency;
- lower investment conversion, contribution, and retention;
- delayed monetization and at least 12 months of liquidity headroom after the modeled trough.

Additional model issues:

- Investment clients are defined as a fixed percentage of current active FX clients rather than a separate acquisition/conversion/retention cohort.
- AUM compounds but has no explicit investor churn or withdrawal schedule.
- The base case reports only 2.8% of Year 3 revenue from investing, so the spreadsheet does not yet demonstrate the stated “investing engine.”

- Revenue is shown, but contribution margin by product and customer concentration are not.

**Required replacement:** rebuild the model around separate customer cohorts, a funded-investor funnel, deposits/withdrawals, contribution margin by product, partner minimums, headcount, regulatory costs, scenario toggles, and a visible checks sheet. Preserve the current workbook as a hypothesis archive until the replacement reconciles to it.

**Exit evidence:** signed pilot report, raw cohort data, revised model, scenario results, and a go/no-go decision.

### Stage 4 — Controlled launch (Months 3–6)

**Goal:** launch only after the legal, operational, and economic evidence supports it.

- Use an approved marketing evidence file and pre-publication compliance review. Performance comparisons must use consistent dates, total-return methodology, dividends, currency, benchmark, and limitations. See the [SEC investment-adviser marketing guide](https://www.sec.gov/resources-small-businesses/small-business-compliance-guides/investment-adviser-marketing).
- Sell diversification, long-term investing discipline, transparency, and fit. Do not state that a home currency or market inevitably destroys wealth or that every client should invest only in the U.S.
- Give each client an investment policy/suitability record and globally diversified portfolio where appropriate. Commercial positioning must not predetermine fiduciary advice.
- Keep tax reporting to one country and label output as informational support, not a filed return or individualized tax advice. Use a rules-version register, source records, reconciliation, review by a qualified local tax professional, error correction, and E&O/cyber coverage.
- Monitor client concentration, complaints, transfer exceptions, partner SLA, bank questions, KYC rejection, AML alerts, funded conversion, retention, AUM flows, and contribution margin monthly.

**Exit evidence:** launch approval, reviewed marketing, client files, operational KPI pack, and incident/complaint register.

## 6. Decision and evidence governance

Use one record for every material closure:

| Field | Required content |
|---|---|
| Decision ID | Stable identifier, for example `LEGAL-BR-001` |
| Issue IDs | One or more of the 45 source issues |
| Owner | One accountable person, even when work is outsourced |
| Decision | What was approved or rejected |
| Evidence | Opinion, contract, registration, test output, data, or policy link |
| Effective date | `YYYY-MM-DD` |
| Assumptions | Facts on which the decision relies |
| Review trigger | Law, country, product, partner, entity, compensation, customer journey, RAUM, incident, or date |
| Next review | Date or event |
| Status | Open / blocked / approved / implemented / verified / superseded |

Minimum recurring cadence:

- **Weekly during Stages 0–3:** gate status, blockers, owner, next evidence.
- **Monthly after launch:** economics, concentration, exceptions, complaints, vendor health, AML/privacy/security incidents.
- **Quarterly:** legal/regulatory change check, partner risk, marketing sample, conflicts, product margin.
- **Annually or on trigger:** RIA/ADV/compliance review, entity/tax review, privacy transfer review, vendor diligence, insurance, business continuity, and full model refresh.

## 8. Definition of ready

The project is ready for a controlled paid launch only when:

- all six stop-sign gates in Section 1 are verified;
- every Stage 0–3 register item is approved, implemented, or explicitly deferred without blocking the launch design;
- there is no unsupported regulatory, tax, performance, pension, estate, or moat claim in customer or investor materials;
- the pilot proves funded-investor conversion and positive contribution margin under an approved cash plan;
- the board has accepted the remaining risks, review triggers, and stop conditions in writing.

Until then, the correct description is: **a commercially interesting thesis in regulatory, operational, and economic validation — not a solved financial platform.**
