How your money moves

Your money, your account. Always.

Your local currency moves through a licensed partner in your country, then into an investment account opened directly in your own name, in the United States. OpenDollars never pools client funds. The account is always yours, never ours.

STEP ONE

Local, licensed

Reais or pesos leave your local bank through a partner licensed to handle that transaction in your country.

STEP TWO

Converted to dollars

The transfer settles in US dollars, documented end to end for both your local authority and ours.

STEP THREE

Held in your name

The account is opened in the United States under your own name. You are the account holder, at every moment.

Why OpenDollars

The part other platforms don't tell you about.

If a non-US citizen dies holding US assets directly, the estate receives only a small exemption before US estate tax applies. Most people find this out at the worst possible time.

$60,000

The entire estate exemption available to a non-US citizen holding US assets directly.

40%

The rate US estate tax can reach on everything above that exemption.

9–18 months

How long heirs can find the account frozen while the estate is settled.

We structure investments to avoid this exposure entirely.

Every OpenDollars account is built so your family never meets the $60,000 cliff, and so the transfer to your heirs isn't held hostage by a foreign probate process.

The same structure also reduces the withholding tax applied to dividends — a quieter cost that compounds against you over a lifetime of investing.
